মোঃ মাজহারুল ইসলাম আকন্দ রবিউল
====================================
(Wikipedia-style Biography Draft — English)

Md. Mazharul Islam Akando Robiul (Bengali: মোঃ মাজহারুল ইসলাম আকন্দ রবিউল; born 21 April 1974) is a Bangladeshi politician and businessman from Jamalpur District, Bangladesh. He is a prominent leader of the Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP) and is currently seeking nomination as the BNP candidate for the Chairman of Sharifpur Union Parishad, Jamalpur Sadar Upazila, Jamalpur.

== Early Life and Education ==

Robiul was born in the historic village of Raghunathpur, Jamalpur Sadar Upazila, Jamalpur District. His father, the late Mozammell Haq Akando, was a founding teacher at Raghunathpur Primary School — a school whose land was donated by his family. His mother was the late Majeda Begum.

He completed his primary education at Raghunathpur Primary School. The school was established in 1972 and upgraded to Raghunathpur High School in 1988. He later pursued higher studies and obtained a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) degree from Government Ashek Mahmud College, Jamalpur.

== Political Career ==

=== Student Politics (1990–1998) ===

In 1990, while studying in Class 10, Robiul joined the popular movement against the autocratic regime of General Ershad. He became associated with the Nandina College Chhatra Dal (student wing of BNP), marking the beginning of his political career.

In 1991, he actively participated in the national parliamentary election campaign in support of the BNP candidate Sirajul Haq and served as the polling center in-charge at Srirampur Bimukhi High School.

In 1993, he was elected as General Secretary of the 2nd Sharifpur Union Chhatra Dal committee. He subsequently served as acting General Secretary for a long period.

In 1995, he contested as candidate for Literature Secretary in the Government Ashek Mahmud College Student Union election.

In 1996, he served as Literature Secretary in the Government Ashek Mahmud College Chhatra Dal committee.

=== National Elections ===

Robiul supported BNP candidates in the national elections of 1991, 1996, and 2001. In the 2001 election, he played a particularly significant role in ensuring BNP's victory in the Jamalpur constituency.

=== Khaleda Zia's Visit (1994) ===

In 1994, then Prime Minister and BNP Chairperson Begum Khaleda Zia visited the Raghunathpur Primary School ground on the occasion of the canal excavation project. Robiul actively discharged his responsibilities during this public gathering — an event he considers a defining chapter of his political life.

=== Union Parishad Politics ===

Robiul has been active in Sharifpur Union Parishad politics for several decades. He contested as Chairman candidate in the 2018 Union Parishad election and secured a notable vote share. He is currently the BNP-nominated candidate for the upcoming Sharifpur Union Parishad election.

== Vision for Sharifpur Union ==

If elected as Chairman, Robiul has committed to:
1. Upgrading primary and secondary school infrastructure
2. Ensuring functional community clinics and free health camps
3. Paving all roads within the Union
4. Supporting farmers with fertilizer, seeds, and training
5. Ensuring safe drinking water and proper sanitation
6. Delivering social safety net benefits fairly to the elderly, widows, and disabled
7. Supporting youth entrepreneurship through microfinance and training
